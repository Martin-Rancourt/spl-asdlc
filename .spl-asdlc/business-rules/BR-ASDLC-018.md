# BR-ASDLC-018 — The consolidation of a round starts only after its angles: its item is blocked by every angle item of the round, each closed by its own reviewer, and no approval counts while an angle item is open.

Status: Enforced

## Rule

The bridge creates the consolidation item with the round, so the whole round is visible from the start, and lays a `blocks` relation from every angle sub-item of the round to it, right after the items and before the round comment and In Review; a relation already there is not written again, an open round missing one gets it at the next tick, and a relation that cannot be read or written is reported and retried, never skipped. The tracker stops counting a relation as blocking once the angle item is resolved (Livela: the `closed` group, `done` or `cancel`, `docs/reference/livela-contract.md` §1; Linear: the blocking issue completed or canceled). In case a dispatcher ignores the relation, a coordinator approval does not count while an angle sub-item of the PR's round (the round at its head, or every round of the PR when none is at its head) is open: the PR is not merged and is told once that it waits for the angle reviews (`docs/reference/04-pr-review.md` §3, §6). Each item of the round is a task with no PR of its own, closed by the agent served it, never by the bridge for a review (ASDLC-WMTX): an angle sub-item by its reviewer once its review is submitted, its body naming the item's key; the consolidation item by its coordinator once its approval or request for changes is submitted, after checking that every angle sub-item of the round has a submitted review naming its key and reopening, instead of approving, the one that has none. A PR waiting on a coordinator's approval at its head with no consolidation item of its own open gets its latest round's consolidation item (the one whose round comment is the ticket's latest for the PR) back to Ready, once per head, with a comment saying why; a canceled one is never reopened. The bridge still closes a round's items left open when its PR resolves (BR-ASDLC-007). The release QA round lays the same relations under its own rule, BR-ASDLC-020 (`.spl-asdlc/open-points.md` #15).

## Evidence

The owner's decision of 2026-11-25, ASDLC-WMTX: an item the bridge closed only at its next tick was served again to the agent that had just delivered it (security reviews, consolidations); a ticket is committed code, a task is work with no code, closed by the agent itself. The owner's decision of 2026-10-09 (option A), after the first real PR review: a dispatcher served the consolidation item at once, and the coordinator approved the PR before the angle reviewer had submitted anything.
