# BR-ASDLC-008 — The state of a ticket follows its PRs, by two rules in order.

Status: Enforced

## Rule

Whenever a PR of a ticket resolves, or a ticket past the merge has an open PR: (1) the review rule has priority, another PR of the ticket in review (opened, not a draft, not sent back) keeps it In Review, a PR sent back by the coordinator puts it In Progress; (2) the completeness rule, with no PR in review a ticket whose boxes (the `## Post deploy` section apart) are not all ticked goes back In Progress; then a draft PR, work in progress, keeps a complete ticket In Progress (REQ-MERGE-028 as corrected); only a ticket with every box ticked, nothing open and a PR merged goes To Deploy. While its PR is open, a ticket is In Review as soon as the PR is opened (not a draft), before CI and its review round, from Backlog, Ready or In Progress with no In Progress on the way, and a draft leaves it where it is (`docs/reference/03-vocabulary.md`, `docs/reference/04-pr-review.md` §1). In Progress is written by the implementer, agent or person, who starts the ticket; the bridge never writes it as a backup when a branch or a PR appears, and moves a ticket to In Progress only by these rules (a send-back, a PR put back in draft before its round, a box left open at a merge, an open PR on a ticket past the merge). A ticket that carries the no-deploy label (`tracker.no_deploy_label`, default `no-deploy`) has nothing to deploy: where the rules say To Deploy, its merge delivers it, Done by the Done gate (Post Deploy with its validation sub-task while only its own `## Post deploy` boxes are open), and no release embarks it. When an open PR's ticket could not be read, nothing is written (`docs/reference/04-pr-review.md` §10).

## Evidence

Owner's decision on several PRs per ticket (2026-10-07). The method (`docs/method.md`, "From ticket to To Deploy"): In Review as soon as the PR is opened, a draft being work in progress, and Done at the merge for a ticket with nothing to deploy. The owner's decision of 2026-10-14: In Progress is set by the implementer who starts the ticket, since a PR appearing means In Review, so the bridge is no backup for it.
