# Consolidate

**Input**: the consolidation item of a PR (review) or of a release candidate (QA), served once every angle has reported. At a PR review the item is blocked by every angle sub-item of its round until each is closed, and an approval you give while one is still open does not count (BR-ASDLC-018). At a release candidate the item is served once every angle report of the candidate is merged: with a tracker that holds implementation blocks it is **Ready** and blocked by each angle sub-item until that one is **To Deploy**; otherwise it waits in **Backlog** and the bridge moves it to **Ready** once every angle report is merged (BR-ASDLC-020). **Output**: at a PR review, every finding answered, retained fixes applied, an approving review, or changes requested; at a release candidate, the verdict, committed in a PR.

## For a PR review

1. Check that every angle sub-item of the round has a submitted review on the PR whose body names its key. An angle with none has not reviewed, even when its sub-item is closed: reopen that sub-item (Ready) with a comment saying no review names it, and stop there; do not approve. Your item comes back to you once it is closed again. Then read every review thread of the PR, from every angle and from humans. Verify every checked box of the ticket outside `## Post deploy` against the diff (the code delivers the criterion; it need not be in effect yet), as the angle reviewers do (`review-pr.md`, step 3): a box checked but not really done is unchecked in the ticket, with a comment naming the box and what is missing; the bridge then returns the PR to draft and the ticket to its implementer.
2. For each finding, decide once: **confirm** (fix it) or **reject** (answer in the thread with the reason). You may change a severity, with the reason. Never leave a thread unanswered.
3. Apply the retained fixes as commits on the PR branch. Keep them small and attributable to their thread. Resolve the threads you handled.
4. If a retained fix is beyond a consolidation (a design change, a missing piece of the contract, a PR that is not deployable on its own or should have been one PR with another, BR-ASDLC-005/006), request changes: the ticket goes back to the implementer, with the list in your review. For a PR that is not self-standing or a split without reason, record the reason in a comment on the ticket too.
5. When every retained fix is in and CI is green at the head, submit an approving review. The bridge merges; you do not.
6. Close your consolidation item yourself (Done) once your approving review or your request for changes is submitted, before you give it back: it is a task, with no PR of its own. If the PR then still waits on an approval at its head (its head moved since, say), the bridge serves the item to you again, once per head.

## For a release candidate

The angle PRs are not yours to approve: the bridge merges each one itself once its report is valid and it carries nothing else (`docs/agents/review-release.md`). You may read one as it comes and request changes at its head when its report is vague or wrong; that holds its merge and sends its sub-item back **In Progress** to its angle. Resolve nothing on the release branch directly.

Your consolidation item is served once every angle report of the candidate is merged (BR-ASDLC-020). Then:

1. Read every merged report of the candidate together, not one by one (`release.record` rendered for the candidate and each angle, `.spl-asdlc/qa/<candidate>/<angle>.json` by default).
2. Judge each finding of every report once: **accepted** (it is real), and then whether it is **blocking** (must not ship), or **rejected**, always with its `reason`. You may change whether an accepted finding is blocking, with the reason. A finding you accept, you do not solve: check that its report captures it as faithfully as it can (`summary`, `anchor`, `evidence`, BR-ASDLC-013), since the bridge copies those three into its ticket and whoever takes it up has nothing else.
3. Check that nothing falls between the angles: a concern no angle owned, or two angles that each assumed the other covered it. Say what you checked, and what you found, in `between_angles`.
4. Write the verdict at `release.record` rendered for the candidate with the key `consolidation` (`.spl-asdlc/qa/<candidate>/consolidation.json` by default; schema and rules in `docs/reference/06-qa-report.md`, "The verdict"): `go` when no accepted finding is blocking, `hold` otherwise. Its `item` is your consolidation item's key, never your name or anyone's (BR-ASDLC-001).
5. Commit it from the branch `qa/<candidate>/consolidation`, push, and open the PR to the release branch; the PR names your consolidation item and carries the verdict and nothing else. Then move your consolidation item to **In Review** yourself, before you give it back: it is a ticket whose code is your verdict, and In Review keeps it from being served to you again while the bridge checks your PR. The bridge merges it when the verdict is valid, every angle PR of the candidate is merged, you are a release coordinator (`release.coordinators`) or one approved it at its head, its required checks are green and it is up to date; what else keeps it from the merge is said once, on the PR. When that is yours to mend (the PR names no item, carries another change or an invalid verdict, or a required check is red), or when no verdict PR is open, the bridge moves your item back **In Progress**, and you are served it again: fix, push, and move it In Review again.

A person who coordinates may give the verdict from the tracker instead: the bridge posts the findings to judge on your consolidation item once every angle report is merged, and commits unchanged the verdict that the item's assignee or the release's owner gives back there in a comment, in a PR of its own (`docs/reference/06-qa-report.md`, "The verdict given in the tracker", rule 2W8R). As an agent, you commit yours in your own PR: a verdict comment of an agent is ignored.

At the merge, the bridge files a ticket for each accepted finding (urgent and assigned to `release.fix_assignee` when blocking, logged for later otherwise), nothing for a rejected one, and moves your consolidation item to **Done**. A `go` verdict lets the GO through, once the gate is green; a `hold` holds it until the blocking fixes, merged to the release branch, make a later candidate, which is reviewed again. Tag that candidate (cut workflow, action `candidate`, dispatched on the release branch, never on `main`) unless the project automates it.

You have the last word on findings and give the candidate's `go` or `hold`; the bridge still computes the GO from the facts, the gate included.
