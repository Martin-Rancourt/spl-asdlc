# 04 — PR review

Fast, on the diff, with several angles and one coordinator. The volume is tens of PRs a day: nothing here may drown the PR or the tracker.

## Invariants

1. **CI first.** A PR is reviewed only when it is ready (not draft), its required checks are green, and its branch is up to date with its base when the base requires it. The content of the CI is the project's (build, tests, lint, project guards); the method only requires a single aggregated required check whose failure or skip blocks.
2. **The ticket's contract gates the review.** The linked ticket must have a complete contract and at least one box ticked (`01-ticket-contract.md`). Otherwise the PR waits and its author is told once.
3. **The bridge opens the review.** When 1 and 2 hold, the bridge sets the ticket **In Review** and creates one **angle sub-item** per configured angle plus one **consolidation item**, all linked to the PR: the PR's **review round**. A round is scoped to its PR: a ticket already In Review for another of its PRs keeps its status and the new PR gets its own round. Which angles apply is configuration: a base set per project, plus angles added by the paths the PR touches (a sensitive path adds the security angle; a UI path adds the design angle).
    **Each item carries its protocol** (BR-ASDLC-015): the agent served it never depends on reading the method's repository, which is absent from the project's. Beside its contract (goal, non-goals, acceptance criteria, business rules), an item says what closes it in the bridge's terms and embeds, under a `## Protocol` section whose headings are demoted below the contract's, the method's protocol of its job: an angle sub-item `docs/agents/review-pr.md` and the angle's file `docs/agents/angles/<angle>.md` (an angle of the project's own, which the method has no file for, gets the generic protocol and a line saying no method notes exist for it); the consolidation item `docs/agents/consolidate.md`. The project's angle notes (`notes` of the angle) stay in the project's repository: the item links them. The texts are compiled into the bridge, so an item carries the protocol of the bridge version that created it, and keeps it: an item is found again by its marker, which holds no protocol text, and is never rewritten. An angle sub-item closes when a submitted review on the PR names its key in its body (§4); the consolidation item closes with the round when the PR resolves (§6, §10).
4. **Findings live in the PR.** Each reviewer reads the diff from its angle, with the ticket's contract and the business rules touched as context, and posts its findings as review comments on the PR (one thread per finding, severity in the first line). A human who comments on the PR is one more angle. The tracker never receives a copy; the sub-item is closed by the bridge when the reviewer's review is submitted, a review whose body names the sub-item's key. The reviewer knows a PR may not close its ticket, and checks that it is deployable on its own (§10); a PR that is not self-standing, or that should have been one PR with another, is a `blocker` finding whose reason the reviewer also records in the ticket's comments.
5. **The coordinator fixes and approves.** Once every angle has submitted, the consolidation item is served. The coordinator reads every thread, rejects false alarms with a reason in the thread, applies the retained fixes as commits on the PR branch, resolves the threads it has handled, and submits an approving review. If the change needed is beyond a fix (a confirmed finding that the PR is not deployable on its own, or should have been one PR, included), it requests changes and the ticket goes back to the implementer (In Progress), unless another PR of the ticket is in review (§10).
6. **CI again, then the bridge merges.** The merge requires: coordinator approval at the head SHA, required checks green at that SHA, no unresolved thread, branch up to date if required, one merge per repository per tick. The merge is a squash by default (configurable), pinned to the head SHA; the branch is deleted; the PR's round closes; the ticket goes where §10 says: **To Deploy** when every box is ticked and no other PR is open.
7. **The merge is idempotent and observable.** The bridge records every merge decision in its action log; a PR merged outside the bridge is swept and given the same transitions, once.
8. **Exclusions.** PRs of the release machinery (release-please PRs, release records, merge-backs) are never reviewed by angles and never merged by this path; `05-release-cycle.md` governs them.
9. **PRs of Apps that need no ticket.** A PR is linked to exactly one ticket, which a human or an agent writes; a PR authored by an App the project lists (`review.ticketless_authors`, for example Dependabot) gets its ticket from the bridge instead. When such a PR is linked to no ticket, the bridge opens one: labelled `ticketless-pr`, with a complete contract whose one box the PR meets, linked to the PR. From the next tick the PR takes the path of every PR (review round, merge, To Deploy, release, closure). Only an App qualifies, never a human whose login matches. When such a PR is closed without merge (an App superseding its own PR), the bridge cancels the ticket it opened and that ticket's open sub-items; a PR reopened later stays on that ticket until a person sets it back to Ready. Any other PR without a ticket waits, and its author is told once.
10. **A ticket and its PRs.** One PR per ticket is the default, with as many commits as needed; several only for a real reason, recorded by the implementer in the ticket's comments. **Every PR is independently deployable**: a merged PR ships with the next release whatever its ticket's state, even when it does not close the ticket; the release embarks the ticket and the closure judges it only once it is To Deploy (`05-release-cycle.md` §11). **A review round is scoped to its PR**: its angle sub-items and its consolidation item close when the PR resolves (Done at a merge, Canceled at a close without merge), while the ticket stays open for a later PR. Whenever a PR of a ticket resolves, and whenever a ticket past the merge (To Deploy, Done) has an open PR, the bridge sets the ticket's state by these rules, in order:
    1. **The review rule** has priority: another PR of the ticket in review (its round open, not sent back) keeps or puts the ticket **In Review**, and that PR's review → deploy cycle runs in full; a PR of the ticket the coordinator sent back (changes requested at its head) puts it **In Progress**.
    2. **The completeness rule**: with no PR in review, a ticket whose boxes are not all ticked (every box of its description under any heading, the `## Post deploy` section set apart; a ticket that declares no box is not complete) goes back **In Progress**. An implementer resumes; its next PR brings the ticket to In Review again, until every box is ticked.
    3. **An open PR on a complete ticket** (REQ-MERGE-028, kept below the two rules): the ticket is **In Review** while that PR waits for its review.
    4. Otherwise, a PR of the ticket merged: **To Deploy**; none merged (a PR closed without merge): **In Progress**.

    A ticket in Post Deploy is not reopened. When the ticket of an open PR could not be read, it may be this ticket's: nothing but "another PR is in review" can be said, so the bridge writes nothing and decides at the next tick.

## What the reviewer receives

- The diff, the PR description, the linked ticket's goal and acceptance criteria, and the ticket's comments, where its implementer records why it has more than one PR (§10).
- The business rules touched by the diff (rows of the registry whose test files or cited paths intersect the diff) and the registry id format.
- Its angle protocol (`docs/agents/review-pr.md` and `docs/agents/angles/<angle>.md`), embedded in its sub-item (§3), and a link to the project's angle notes if any.
- The severity scale and the verdict vocabulary (`03-vocabulary.md`).

## What the coordinator receives

Everything the reviewers received, plus every review thread, the CI status, and the consolidation protocol (`docs/agents/consolidate.md`, embedded in the consolidation item, §3): every finding is confirmed or rejected with a reason, exactly once; a rejected finding is answered in its thread; a confirmed one is fixed on the branch or sent back to the implementer.

## Configuration (per project)

- Base angles and path-triggered angles, each with an optional assignee hint.
- Merge method (`squash` | `merge`), whether the branch must be up to date, the diff size above which an extra angle is added.
- Whether a human must approve in addition to the coordinator (policy `human-approval: always | sensitive-paths | never`).
- The Apps whose PRs need no ticket (§9).
